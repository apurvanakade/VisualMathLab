Interactive, browser-based visualizations of numerical methods.

Visual Math Lab

A picture is worth a thousand equations.

Here you’ll find root finders, ODE solvers, splines, filters and the odd spirograph, all running live in your browser. Change the inputs and watch what the method does. Apps come loaded with examples, including ones where things go wrong, because seeing a method fail teaches as much as seeing it work.
